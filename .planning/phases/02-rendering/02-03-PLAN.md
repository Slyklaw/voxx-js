<objective>
Implement chunk loading hysteresis, distance thresholds, throttling, and object pooling to prevent jitter and stutter during gameplay.

Purpose: Naive chunk loading causes visual jitter at boundaries (chunks rapidly load/unload) and stutter (loading too many chunks per frame). Hysteresis creates a buffer zone, throttling spreads work across frames, and pooling reduces garbage collection pressure.

Output: ChunkManager loads chunks at VIEW_DISTANCE, unloads at VIEW_DISTANCE + HYSTERESIS_MARGIN, limits CHUNKS_PER_FRAME loads, and reuses chunk objects from pool.
</objective>
